Methodology

Intel(R) Core(TM) i7-8750H CPU @ 2.20GHz; 12 logical processors; 63.92 GiB physical RAM; Windows 11 amd64; Oracle Corporation JDK 27 (27+35-2325).

Two separately warmed JVM forks per case; two 100 ms warmups and three 200 ms measurements per fork; one JMH worker; requested compute threads are shown per profile. Fork heap: 256 MiB initial / 1 GiB maximum. CPU affinity was not set. Native worker counts were not independently audited. These short measurements may have wide confidence intervals; rankings are machine-specific.

The benchmark adapters and launcher are available in the benchmark source. Input and output validation occur before timing; the original ZIP preserves correctness, pivot, fairness, origin and expanded rectangular preflight logs. Public data contains timings, samples and hardware specifications; local paths and raw logs are excluded.

For rectangular multiplication, m × k × n means (m × k) · (k × n). Timings and confidence intervals are copied from JMH without rounding before plotting. Ratios compare means for the same profile, operation and size; ratio confidence intervals are not estimated.

Profiles are published only when present in the saved measurements. Machine-capacity profiles use JVM-visible logical processors. Commons block results are a separate implementation variant. Worker counts were not independently audited. Compare the same operation, dimensions and requested-thread profile. The Commons snapshot differs between the October 20 and October 28 runs, so cross-run changes cannot be attributed solely to NM Dev.
